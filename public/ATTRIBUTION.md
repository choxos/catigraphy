# Attribution

Every surface and every slice in this atlas comes from CT data published on MorphoSource by
3D Anatomy Studios. Nothing here is redrawn, and nothing here is modeled: the geometry is a
threshold through the published volumes and the slice viewer serves the volumes themselves.

## Terms

The MorphoSource records carry the rights statement
[In Copyright, Non-Commercial Use Permitted](http://rightsstatements.org/vocab/InC-NC/1.0/) under
MorphoSource's standard use agreement. Across the twelve series the agreement permits 3D use and
does not permit commercial use, and it asks that derivatives published from these media be
archived on MorphoSource. This atlas is a non-commercial educational resource. Anyone
redistributing the surfaces it builds should read the per-series terms below and archive the
derivatives as the agreement asks.
